# Medical news source notes

The in-game ticker rewrites and anonymizes themes rather than reproducing article text or identifying private people.

- National Health Commission of China: https://www.nhc.gov.cn/
- State Council healthcare-system policy (2023-03-23): https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2023-03/23/content_5748063.htm
- National Healthcare Security Administration: https://www.nhsa.gov.cn/
- Supreme People's Court public portal and judgment themes: https://www.court.gov.cn/
- PubMed retractions and research-integrity literature index: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/

The visiting-scholar sexual-misconduct ticker is an anonymized composite inspired by publicly reported overseas criminal and university-discipline cases. Search results available in this environment did not support responsibly attributing a specific medical scholar, country, institution, or alleged motive. The game therefore omits all identifying details and does not repeat the unverified claim that pornography caused the conduct.
